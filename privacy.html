Privacy Policy

Effective 10 November 2026

In short: Eatlas has no account, no advertising and no tracking. Your diary and your profile stay on your iPhone. When you analyze a meal photo, the photo is sent to our server and to OpenAI to estimate its nutrition, only after you agree, and neither we nor OpenAI keep it beyond what is described below.

Who we are

Eatlas is made by Nikita Storchai, an individual developer. Questions about this policy: storschajnikita@gmail.com.

What stays on your iPhone

Eatlas keeps these on your device only. None of it is sent to us.

  • Your profile: the name you choose to enter, sex, age, height, weight, activity level and goal, and the daily targets worked out from them.
  • Your diary: the meals you log, with their calories, protein, fat, carbs and ingredients.
  • Weekly summaries written by the coach.
  • Settings and choices, such as units and whether you agreed to photo analysis.

The widgets read a short summary of today's numbers that the app writes into storage shared with them on the same iPhone. Like other app data, your diary can be included in your iPhone's own backups (iCloud or computer), under your control and Apple's privacy terms.

Meal photos

The first time you analyze a photo, Eatlas explains where it goes and asks for your agreement. If you agree:

  • The photo is made smaller (at most 768 pixels on a side) and its location and camera details are removed on your iPhone.
  • It is sent over an encrypted connection to our server, a Google Cloud function run through Firebase in the United States, which passes it to OpenAI to estimate the meal's nutrition and returns the result to your iPhone.
  • We do not store the photo. We ask OpenAI not to store the request either; OpenAI may still keep it for up to 30 days to detect abuse, under OpenAI's API data policy, and does not use it to train its models.
  • Eatlas does not save the photo on your iPhone; only the meal you confirm goes into your diary.

You can use Eatlas without photo analysis: the barcode scanner and logging a meal again need no photo.

Counting daily scans

Photo analysis is limited per day. To count it, our server keeps a one-way hash of the App Store transaction identifier of your copy of the app together with the day's count, for about three days. It cannot be turned back into the identifier and is not linked to your name or Apple Account.

Keeping the server for the real app

To make sure requests come from the genuine Eatlas app, every photo analysis carries a short-lived token from Apple's App Attest, checked through Google's Firebase App Check. These tokens prove the app is genuine; they do not identify you. Like any server, ours and its provider record technical logs of requests (for example the time and the result), which are kept for a limited time to keep the service running and secure.

Barcodes

When you scan a barcode, Eatlas sends only the barcode number to Open Food Facts, an open food database, to look up the product's nutrition.

The coach

The weekly summary is written by Apple Intelligence on your iPhone. Your meals are not sent anywhere for it.

Camera and photos

Eatlas uses the camera to photograph meals and read barcodes, after asking for permission. Photos you pick from your library are chosen through Apple's photo picker, so Eatlas sees only the photo you pick.

What we share

Only what is described above, with OpenAI, Google (Firebase and Google Cloud) and Open Food Facts, and only to provide the feature you use. We never sell, rent or share information about you for advertising, and Eatlas contains no advertising or analytics code.

Deleting your data

You can delete any meal in the app. Deleting Eatlas removes your diary and profile from your iPhone. The scan count on our server expires on its own after about three days.

Health information

Calories and macros from a photo are estimates. Eatlas is not a medical device and does not give medical advice.

Children

Eatlas is meant for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
