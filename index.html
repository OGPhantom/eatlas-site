Snap your plate.

Take a photo of your meal. Eatlas finds what's on the plate and estimates the calories, protein, fat and carbs. Up to three meal photos a day.

Every ingredient, weighed.

Each ingredient comes with its weight. If a guess is off, fix the name, the weight or the calories before you save.

Packaged food? Scan it.

Point the camera at the barcode. Eatlas reads the label from Open Food Facts, and you set how much you had.

Your usual meal in one tap.

Meals you log often wait under Log again. One tap adds them, even after your three photos for the day.

Always know what's left.

Your daily targets come from your height, weight, activity and goal. Widgets keep the rest of today on your Home Screen and Lock Screen.

Your month at a glance.

Every day you logged is marked on the calendar. Tap one to see what you ate and how it added up.

A weekly coach on your iPhone.

On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Eatlas reads your last week and suggests what to change. It runs on the device itself.

Your diary stays on your iPhone.

Read the privacy policy
No account
No sign-up, no email, no password. Open the app and take a photo.
Photos, only if you agree
A meal photo is sent for analysis only after you allow it, and Eatlas doesn't keep it.
Barcodes
Only the barcode number goes to Open Food Facts, an open food database.
The coach
Your weekly summary is written on your iPhone by Apple Intelligence.
No ads, no tracking
Eatlas shows no advertising and doesn't follow what you do.
