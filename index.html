Your day and your week

  • Daily targets for calories and macros, worked out from your age, height, weight, activity and goal.
  • A diary with a month calendar and a search through every meal.
  • A weekly summary from a coach that runs on your iPhone, on devices with Apple Intelligence.
  • Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets with today's numbers.

Private by design

No account and no sign-up. Your diary stays on your iPhone. A meal photo is sent for analysis only after you agree, and Eatlas never stores it. The details are in the privacy policy.
